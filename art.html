About

The Unleashed draws people from photos, characters from film, anime and myth, and the odd original.

Most of the work is made in Procreate: flat grounds, clean shapes, and a figure that melts away at the bottom of the frame. The sketchbook is where the slower drawing happens, in graphite and ink.

New pieces go to Instagram first. A few speed-draws are on YouTube as Shorts, next to the chess and coding videos. See everything The Unleashed makes.
